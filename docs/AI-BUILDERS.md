# Cortex Student direction

Cortex Study Room is the multi-agent runtime prototype. Cortex Student is the broader adaptive learning product direction.

The current step removes the Physics-only assumption from the study-room experience. A learner can enter a subject and topic in natural language. Cortex identifies the subject when it has a deterministic curriculum profile, maps concepts and prerequisites, teaches, reviews and assesses. Unknown subjects fall back to a transparent generic structure instead of fabricating syllabus knowledge.

The architecture is intended to grow through grounded curriculum ingestion: syllabus, notes, textbook or teacher-provided material can supply the concept graph and assessment bank while the adaptive learner model remains shared.
